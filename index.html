About Me

I am a master's student at the School of Software and Microelectronics, Peking University, specializing in Artificial Intelligence. My research interests focus on Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI Agents.

I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Central South University in 2024. During my undergraduate studies, I built a solid foundation in computer science through courses including Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Data Structures, Algorithms, Machine Learning, and Computer Vision.

Currently, I am exploring the boundaries of AI capabilities, particularly in developing intelligent agents that can effectively interact with and reason about the world. I am passionate about bridging the gap between academic research and practical applications.
